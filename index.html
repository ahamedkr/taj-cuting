Our Story

A Legacy of
Craft & Quality

Founded in 1998, Taj Cutting Meat Shop began with a simple promise — to deliver the freshest, finest halal meat to every family's table. What started as a small neighbourhood butcher has grown into a beloved institution, trusted by thousands.

Every piece of meat we handle passes through the hands of master butchers who have spent years perfecting their craft. We believe that great food starts with great ingredients, and great ingredients start with us.

100% Halal Certified – All animals slaughtered according to Islamic principles
Farm to Shop – Direct sourcing from trusted local and international farms
Custom Cuts – Tell us exactly how you want it, we deliver perfection
Daily Fresh Stock – Never frozen, always fresh delivered every morning
